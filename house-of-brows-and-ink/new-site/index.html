Questions

Asked often.

Does it hurt?

Most clients describe it as light scratching — a topical numbing agent is used throughout the appointment to keep you comfortable. Many clients relax or even doze off in the chair.

How long do ombré brows and lip blush last?

Results are semi-permanent and typically last 1–3 years depending on your skin type, sun exposure, and lifestyle. Affordable color boosts at 6–12 months ($75) keep everything crisp.

What is the healing process like?

Expect the color to look darker for the first several days, then soften as skin heals over 4–6 weeks. You'll leave with full written aftercare instructions, and your included 6–8 week touch-up perfects the final result.

I'm nervous — can I just talk to you first?

Absolutely. Consultations are free, 25 minutes, and pressure-free. We'll cover your goals, coloring, contraindications, and healing so you can decide with confidence. A $25 patch test is also available if you have sensitive skin.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes — a small deposit ($25–$50 depending on the service) secures your appointment and is applied toward your total on the day.
